Clere

Clere is a personal productivity app that brings your calendar, messages, and tasks together in one place.

Clere connects to Google Calendar, Notion, Slack, and Apple Calendar to surface what matters most — upcoming events, messages that need replies, and tasks due today.

All data is processed on-device. Clere does not store your calendar or message data on external servers.

###[Clere] Privacy Policy **Last Updated: April 28, 2026** Clere (hereinafter "the Service") highly values the protection of users' personal information and complies with relevant international data protection laws. This Privacy Policy is designed to transparently inform you of how the Service handles your data. ### 1. Information Collection and Methods The Service accesses and utilizes the following information only with your explicit consent to provide its core features: - **Google (Gmail/Calendar):** Read access to summarize email content and sync calendar events. - **Notion:** Read access to summarize pages and database content. - **Slack:** Read access to retrieve and summarize workspace messages. - **Apple:** Access to iCloud calendar data via App-Specific Passwords. ### 2. Purpose of Use The accessed data is used strictly for the following purposes: - **LLM-based Summarization:** Providing a concise overview of integrated messenger and document content. - **Integrated Viewer:** Consolidating scattered schedules and messages into a single user interface. ### 3. Data Storage and Retention (Core Principle) - **Zero-Server Storage Policy:** The Service **does not store** original messages or email content on any remote servers. All summarization processes occur in real-time. Once the task is completed, the original data is immediately purged from memory. - **On-Device Persistence:** Summarized results and metadata are stored **locally on your device's secure storage** (AsyncStorage/SecureStore). We do not maintain a central database of your personal information. - **Authentication Tokens:** Access tokens for third-party integrations are encrypted and stored solely within the user's device (SecureStore). These tokens are never transmitted to or stored on Clere’s infrastructure. ### 4. Third-Party Service Integration To provide summarization features, the Service communicates directly with official APIs (Google, Slack, Notion). No data is shared with unauthorized third parties, and all transmissions are encrypted via SSL/TLS. ### 5. User Rights and Data Deletion - Users have the right to withdraw consent for data collection at any time using the **'Disconnect'** feature within the app. - Upon disconnection or account reset, all authentication tokens and locally cached data are **immediately and permanently deleted** from the device. ### 6.
